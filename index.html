## How he runs AI spend

He runs AI spend like a staged fund. Each use case passes five gates, and money is released at each one:

1. Baseline: what today's process costs, how long it takes and how often it's right.
2. Owner: a named person in the business who'll use it and answer for the result.
3. Target: the accuracy it has to reach on the company's own cases, agreed before anything is built.
4. Supervised: a person checks the AI's work until it hits that target.
5. Unattended: only then does it run on its own, measured on cost per correct outcome against the old way.

Run AI spend like a staged fund.

Most large companies, groups and portfolios have hundreds of possible AI use cases, a handful of pilots and very little in production. What’s missing is a way to decide which few matter to the P&L and when to stop the rest. I use metered funding against evidence: every use case earns its next tranche of money by proving it works.
