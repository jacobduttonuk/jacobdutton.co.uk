Run AI spend like a staged fund.

Most large companies have hundreds of possible AI use cases, a handful of pilots and very little in production. What’s missing is a way to decide which few matter to the P&L and when to stop the rest. I run it the way I ran ADQ’s innovation fund: every use case earns the next tranche of money with evidence.

Gate 1

Baseline

What today’s process costs, how long it takes and how often it’s right.

Gate 2

Owner

A named person in the business who’ll use it and answer for the result.

Gate 3

Target

The accuracy it has to reach on the company’s own cases, agreed before anything is built.

Gate 4

Supervised

A person checks the AI’s work until it hits that target, case after case.

Gate 5

Unattended

Only then does it run on its own, measured on cost per correct outcome against the old way.

Money released at each gateThe rest get stopped early and cheaply
